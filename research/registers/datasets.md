# datasets.md

Datasets and content corpora usable for training, validation, benchmarking, or as grounding content, per framework section 9. Most program corpora are content/reference sets (Concept B) rather than training datasets; no purpose-built training dataset (older adults in home settings, or pregnant people) was licensed in any phase.
